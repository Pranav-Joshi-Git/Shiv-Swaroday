# Glossary

[↩ Back to overview](00-overview.md)

Alphabetical definitions of key terms from Shiv Swarodaya.

---

**Agni** — Fire tattva; rising, transforming quality of breath.

**Akasha** — Ether/space tattva; subtle, spacious quality of breath.

**Angula** — Finger-width; unit measuring breath-flow distance from nostril (typically 4, 8, 12, or 16 angulas).

**Apana** — One of five primary vayus; governs downward/eliminative functions, seat at anus.

**Atman** — Individual self/soul; ultimately identical with Paramatma in the liberation path.

**Bhukti** — Worldly mastery; using svara knowledge to predict and navigate outcomes (war, conception, disease, weather).

**Devadatta** — One of five subsidiary vayus; governs yawning.

**Dhananjaya** — One of five subsidiary vayus; pervades entire body, remains after death.

**Ghadi** (also Ghatika) — Time unit equal to 24 minutes; 60 ghadis = 1 day.

**Ham-Sa** — Reverse form of So-Ham; the natural sound of exhalation-inhalation (Ham = out, Sa = in).

**Ida** — Left/lunar nadi; flows in left nostril, cool/receptive polarity, associated with moon and feminine principle.

**Jala** — Water tattva; flowing, adhesive quality of breath.

**Jiva** — Living being; individual soul embodied in material form.

**Krikara** — One of five subsidiary vayus; governs sneezing and hunger.

**Kumbhaka** — Breath retention; middle phase of pranayama between inhalation and exhalation.

**Kundalini** — Dormant spiritual energy coiled at base of spine; awakened through yogic practice.

**Kurma** — One of five subsidiary vayus; governs blinking.

**Moksha** — Liberation; reabsorption of individual self (Atman) into supreme self (Paramatma).

**Mukti** — Liberation path; using svara control (pranayama) for spiritual transformation and transcendence.

**Nadi** — Channel carrying prana through the body; text states 72,000 total, with Ida, Pingala, and Sushumna as primary three.

**Naga** — One of five subsidiary vayus; governs belching and hiccups.

**Paksha** — Lunar fortnight; Shukla Paksha (waxing/bright half), Krishna Paksha (waning/dark half).

**Paramatma** — Supreme self; formless source from which tattvas emanate and to which liberated beings return.

**Pingala** — Right/solar nadi; flows in right nostril, hot/active polarity, associated with sun and masculine principle.

**Poorna** — Full breath; flowing strongly and smoothly through one nostril.

**Prana** — Life force animating the body; also name of one of five primary vayus governing inhalation and heart region.

**Pranayama** — Yogic breath control; systematically manipulating inhalation (puraka), retention (kumbhaka), and exhalation (rechaka).

**Prashna** — Question/divination; predicting outcomes based on questioner's position, timing, and practitioner's breath-state at moment of inquiry.

**Prithvi** — Earth tattva; stable, heavy, solid quality of breath.

**Puraka** — Inhalation; first phase of pranayama.

**Rechaka** — Exhalation; final phase of pranayama.

**Sahasrara** — Crown chakra at top of head; destination of prana-apana union rising through Sushumna in yogic practice.

**Samadhi** — Deep meditative absorption; text specifies 3 hours (one yama) of thoughtless state increases lifespan by 72 minutes daily.

**Samana** — One of five primary vayus; governs digestion and assimilation, seat at navel.

**Shanmukhi Mudra** — "Six gates" practice; closing mouth, nostrils, ears, and eyes to perceive tattva attributes internally.

**Shoonya** — Empty/void breath; weak, irregular, or absent flow through a nostril.

**Siddhi** — Perfection/supernatural power; mastery achieved through practice.

**So-Ham** — Natural breath mantra; "I am That" (So = inhalation sound, Ham = exhalation sound).

**Sushumna** — Central nadi running along spine; path for prana-apana union in yogic practice, adverse for worldly actions.

**Svara** — Observable breath-flow through nostrils; interface for reading prana/nadi/tattva states.

**Tattva** — Fundamental cosmic principle/element; five tattvas (earth, water, fire, air, ether) emanate from Paramatma, compose body, and manifest as breath-qualities.

**Udana** — One of five primary vayus; governs upward movement and speech, seat at throat.

**Vayu** — Air tattva; moving, dispersing quality of breath; also refers to the 10 subsidiary pranas (5 primary + 5 subsidiary vayus).

**Vyana** — One of five primary vayus; governs circulation throughout body.

**Yama** — Time period of 3 hours (8 yamas = 24-hour day).
